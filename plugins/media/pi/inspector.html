Automatic: the player last chosen with a double press, or the one that started playing last (shared by all pages). A fixed choice (e.g. Spotify) is not changed by a double press while it is running.

Dots at the top of the tile = active players (orange = playing, wide = shown). When a double press has something to do, pause / resume waits 0.3 s for a second press.

A short press always pauses / resumes.

Shared by all Media tiles. When off, covers come only from local files (e.g. the browser); otherwise a gradient is shown. Downloaded covers are kept in ~/.cache/opendeck-hub/covers.
